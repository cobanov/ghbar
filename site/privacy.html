Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

GHBar collects no data

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no accounts of our own. GHBar has no server: the app talks to GitHub and to nothing else.

What stays on your Mac

Your GitHub token (in the macOS Keychain), the record of which items you've seen, your settings, and cached profile pictures. None of it leaves your Mac.

What GHBar sends to GitHub

The searches needed to list the pull requests and issues on your repositories, sent to api.github.com with your token, and requests for profile pictures from GitHub's image server. That traffic is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions

The whole app is open source: read the code or open an issue.
